# FlipRules

Ranks buy → transform → sell actions on the WoW Forever auction house, so you know which ones make money right now.

## Flips

**Flip**:
One repeatable action that turns bought items into items you sell, judged by its cost, its value after the AH cut, and a verdict.
_Avoid_: Rule (that's the code's word), deal, trade

**Writ flip**:
A flip that opens a sealed writ and sells the Craftsman's Writ that drops from it.
_Avoid_: Sealed rule, open flip

**Craft flip**:
A flip that buys reagents, crafts one learned recipe, and sells the product on the AH. It may be linked to the Craftsman's Writ that asks for its product.
_Avoid_: Recipe flip, LW rule, craft rule

**Writ link**:
The connection between a craft flip and the Craftsman's Writ that asks for its product, which adds that writ's station share to the cost.
_Avoid_: Writ craft

## Crafting

**Learned recipe**:
A recipe one of your characters has learned. Only learned recipes become craft flips.
_Avoid_: Known recipe, available recipe

**Crafter**:
Your one leatherworking character, whose learned recipes are the craft flips.
_Avoid_: Owner, alt

**Intermediate**:
A reagent you can also craft yourself, such as Heavy Leather, so its cost is the cheaper of buying and crafting it.
_Avoid_: Sub-recipe, chain item

**Station**:
A placeable item that a writ tier's craft needs, used up when placed and spread over the crafts done with it.
_Avoid_: Camp item, tool
